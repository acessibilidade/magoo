  • Tables

    Organize data in rows and columns, preserving relationships between cells and headers.

  • Headings

    Establish content hierarchy and help people locate and understand page sections.

  • Tabbar

    A bar providing access to an interface's main areas, with a current destination indicator and consistent navigation.

  • Cards

    Group content about a subject with clear hierarchy and distinctions between information, navigation, and actions.

  • Page regions

    Organize the header, main content, navigation, complementary content, and footer to support reading and page access.

  • Layout and content order

    Organizes groups, reading, and navigation across screen sizes while preserving relationships, context, and access to controls.
